Privacy Policy – El Dato Raro

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Who uses the app

El Dato Raro is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish videos on their own TikTok account.

2. Data we access

When the owner connects their TikTok account, the app receives:

  • Basic profile information (display name), to show which account is connected.
  • An access token, used to upload and publish videos on that account.

3. How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's local computer. They are never sent to any server other than TikTok's own API.

4. Data we do not collect

The app does not collect, store or process data from other TikTok users, viewers or followers. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

5. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Revoking it stops the app from accessing the account.

6. Contact

Open an issue at github.com/ajrpintado15/ajrpintado15.github.io/issues.
